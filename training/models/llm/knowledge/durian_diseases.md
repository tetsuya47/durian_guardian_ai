# Durian Diseases Guide

## Anthracnose (Colletotrichum gloeosporioides)

**Symptoms:**
- Small, water-soaked spots on leaves that enlarge into irregular brown lesions
- Lesions have concentric rings and yellow halos
- Leaf curling and defoliation in severe cases
- Fruit infections cause sunken, dark lesions

**Conditions:**
- High humidity (>90%)
- Temperatures 25-30°C
- Extended wet periods

**Treatment:**
- Copper-based fungicides
- Azoxystrobin or Mancozeb application
- Prune infected branches
- Improve air circulation

## Canker (Phytophthora palmivora / Botryodiplodia theobromae)

**Symptoms:**
- Dark, sunken lesions on stems and branches
- Gum exudation (amber-colored sap)
- Wilting and dieback of branches
- Tree death in severe cases

**Conditions:**
- Poor drainage
- Wound sites on tree
- Wet season

**Treatment:**
- Cut out infected tissue
- Apply Bordeaux paste
- Improve soil drainage
- Metalaxyl systemic fungicide

## Pink Disease (Corticium salmonicolor / Erythricium salmonicolor)

**Symptoms:**
- Pinkish-white fungal growth on branches
- Branch dieback
- Leaves turn brown but remain attached
- Pink crust on bark surface

**Conditions:**
- High humidity
- Overlapping branches
- Dense canopy

**Treatment:**
- Remove and burn infected branches
- Apply copper fungicide
- Prune for better air flow
- Reduce nitrogen fertilization

## Sooty Mold (Capnodium spp.)

**Symptoms:**
- Black, powdery coating on leaves
- Grows on honeydew from insects
- Reduced photosynthesis
- Leaf yellowing and drop

**Conditions:**
- Presence of scale insects, mealybugs, aphids
- Poor pest management

**Treatment:**
- Control insect pests first
- Wash with mild soap solution
- Potassium bicarbonate spray
- Improve tree vigor

## Stem Cracking / Gummosis

**Symptoms:**
- Longitudinal cracks on trunk and branches
- Gum exudation from cracks
- Bark peeling
- Secondary infection risk

**Causes:**
- Water stress (flooding/drought)
- Sunburn damage
- Nutritional imbalances
- Mechanical injury

**Treatment:**
- Avoid waterlogging
- Whitewash trunk against sunburn
- Balanced fertilizer application
- Seal cracks with Bordeaux paste
