# User Guide — Farmers & Technicians

Welcome to Durian Guardian AI (Hapii Green)! Follow this guide to analyze tree foliage and view care recommendations.

## 1. Performing AI Leaf Diagnosis
1. Open the app and navigate to the **Disease Detection** tab.
2. Select **Camera** to capture a leaf photo or **Gallery** to pick a sample template.
3. The app will perform an automated image quality check. If the photo is blurry or dark, follow the instructions to recapture.
4. Once verified, the AI runs classification. The screen will render:
   - **Disease Prediction**: The name of the detected disease and classification confidence.
   - **Explainability (Grad-CAM)**: Toggle the **AI Heatmap** switch to view exactly which spots the model focused on.
   - **Recommendations**: Immediate treatment recommendations, urgency level, and estimated loss projection.

## 2. Viewing History logs
- Select the **History** icon at the top right of the screen to browse historical diagnostic scans.
