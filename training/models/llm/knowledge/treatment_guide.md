# Durian Treatment Guide

## General Treatment Principles

1. **Early detection** - Monitor trees weekly during wet season
2. **Cultural control first** - Prune, improve drainage, balanced nutrition
3. **Chemical control** - Use registered fungicides, rotate modes of action
4. **Biological control** - Trichoderma spp. for soil-borne diseases
5. **Record keeping** - Document all treatments and outcomes

## Fungicide Rotation Schedule

| Active Ingredient | Group | Use Limit | Target Diseases |
|---|---|---|---|
| Azoxystrobin | QoI | 2x/year | Anthracnose, Pink Disease |
| Mancozeb | Dithiocarbamate | 4x/year | Anthracnose, Canker |
| Copper Oxychloride | Multi-site | 6x/year | Pink Disease, Canker |
| Metalaxyl + Mancozeb | Phenylamide | 2x/year | Phytophthora |
| Propiconazole | DMI | 2x/year | Stem Rot, Pink Disease |

## Organic Treatment Options

- Neem oil (2%) - Insect control, mild fungicide
- Bacillus subtilis - Biological fungicide
- Trichoderma harzianum - Soil health
- Compost tea - General tree health
- Potassium bicarbonate (1%) - Powdery mildew
- Copper soap - Bacterial diseases

## Season-Based Calendar

**Dry Season (March-June):**
- Prune dead/diseased branches
- Apply lime to soil (2kg/tree)
- Monitor for stem cracking
- Whitewash trunks

**Wet Season (July-November):**
- Weekly fungicide spray (alternate products)
- Improve drainage
- Monitor for pink disease
- Remove infected fruits

**Harvest Season (December-February):**
- Stop chemical spray 14 days before harvest
- Monitor fruit rot
- Apply calcium to improve fruit quality
- Post-harvest tree nutrition
