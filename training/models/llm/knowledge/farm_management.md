# Durian Farm Management Guide

## Orchard Establishment

- **Spacing**: 10m x 10m (100 trees/hectare)
- **Soil pH**: 5.5-6.5
- **Organic matter**: >3% before planting
- **Drainage**: Raised beds in wet areas
- **Wind protection**: Plant windbreak trees

## Nutrition Management

**Fertilizer Schedule (per tree per year):**
- Nitrogen (N): 500-800g
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 200-400g
- Potassium (K2O): 600-1200g
- Magnesium (MgO): 100-200g

**Application Timing:**
- 50% after harvest (February-March)
- 30% mid-season (June-July)
- 20% pre-fruiting (September-October)

**Micronutrients:**
- Boron: 50g/tree/year (prevent fruit cracking)
- Zinc: 30g/tree/year
- Calcium: Prevent fruit rot

## Water Management

- **Young trees**: 20-40L/tree/week
- **Mature trees**: 100-200L/tree/week
- **Fruiting season**: Do not let soil dry completely
- **Dry season**: Irrigate 2-3 times/week
- **Rainy season**: Ensure good drainage

## Pest Management

**Common Pests:**
- Durian fruit borer (Tirathaba spp.)
- Leaf-eating caterpillar
- Mealybugs
- Scale insects
- Thrips
- Mites

**IPM Strategy:**
1. Monitor weekly
2. Use yellow sticky traps
3. Biological control (parasitoid wasps)
4. Selective insecticides only when threshold reached
5. Maintain beneficial insect habitat
