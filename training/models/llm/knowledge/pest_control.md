# Durian Pest Control Guide

## Mealybug (Planococcus minor, Pseudococcus spp.)

**Identification:**
- White, waxy bodies, 3-5mm long
- Found on leaf undersides, stems, fruits
- Produce honeydew → sooty mold

**Damage:**
- Leaf yellowing and wilting
- Fruit deformation
- Reduced photosynthesis (from sooty mold)

**Control:**
- **Biological**: Cryptolaemus montrouzieri, parasitic wasps
- **Organic**: Neem oil (2%), insecticidal soap
- **Chemical**: Thiamethoxam, Imidacloprid (soil drench)
- **Cultural**: Prune infested branches, ant control

## Thrips (Thrips hawaiiensis, Frankliniella spp.)

**Identification:**
- Tiny (1-2mm), slender, yellow-brown insects
- Silvery streaks/scars on leaves
- Distorted new growth
- Rusty appearance on fruits

**Damage:**
- Leaf scarring and curling
- Fruit scarring (economic loss)
- Bud drop

**Control:**
- **Biological**: Predatory mites (Amblyseius spp.)
- **Organic**: Neem oil, spinosad
- **Chemical**: Spinetoram, Abamectin
- **Cultural**: Blue sticky traps, reflective mulch

## Durian Fruit Borer (Tirathaba rufivena)

**Identification:**
- Pinkish-brown caterpillar, 20-30mm
- Bore into flowers and young fruits
- Frass visible at entry holes

**Damage:**
- Flower drop
- Fruit deformation and drop
- Secondary fungal infection

**Control:**
- **Biological**: Trichogramma egg parasitoids
- **Chemical**: Chlorantraniliprole, Cyantraniliprole
- **Cultural**: Bag fruits, remove fallen fruits
- **Monitoring**: Pheromone traps

## Scale Insects (Aspidiotus spp., Coccus spp.)

**Identification:**
- Small (2-5mm), immobile bumps on stems/leaves
- Hard or soft shells
- Honeydew production

**Control:**
- **Biological**: Chilocorus ladybirds, parasitic wasps
- **Organic**: Horticultural oil (dormant season)
- **Chemical**: Buprofezin, Pyriproxyfen (IGR)
- **Cultural**: Prune heavily infested branches

## Mites (Eriophyidae, Tetranychidae)

**Identification:**
- Microscopic (0.1-1mm)
- Stippling/yellowing of leaves
- Fine webbing (spider mites)
- Gall formation (eriophyid mites)

**Control:**
- **Biological**: Predatory mites (Phytoseiulus persimilis)
- **Organic**: Sulfur spray, neem oil
- **Chemical**: Abamectin, Fenpyroximate
- **Cultural**: Maintain humidity, avoid dusty conditions
