# 🌳 DURIAN GUARDIAN AI (DGA)
> **AI Operating System for Smart Durian Farms**  
> 🛡️ *Protect Every Tree. Predict Every Risk.*

---

## 📌 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

**Durian Guardian AI (DGA)** is an artificial intelligence platform designed to support health management and operation of smart durian farms.

Unlike conventional applications that only detect diseases via image recognition, DGA combines **Computer Vision, Weather Intelligence, and Tree History** to comprehensively assess risks, assist in decision-making, and manage entire farms driven by data.

The goal of DGA is not to replace farmers, but to act as an **AI Farm Manager** – an AI assistant helping managers make faster, more precise, and data-driven decisions rather than relying solely on subjective experience.

---

## ⚠️ 2. REAL-WORLD PROBLEMS (PAIN POINTS)

Currently, managing large-scale durian orchards still faces major limitations:

- 🚨 **Late Detection:** Diseases are often discovered only when they have reached an advanced stage.
- 👨‍🌾 **Over-reliance on Experience:** Lack of standardized diagnosis methods.
- ❌ **Missing Health Records:** No historical tracking for individual trees.
- 🌦️ **Ignoring Environmental Factors:** Inability to predict disease outbreak risks based on weather conditions.
- 📉 **Inefficient Resource Allocation:** Difficulty identifying high-risk zones to prioritize inspection.

**Consequences:** Increased treatment costs, higher tree mortality rates, lower yields, and reduced management efficiency.

---

## 💡 3. THE DGA SOLUTION

Durian Guardian AI builds a comprehensive **AI Farm Manager** capable of:

Observation → Analysis → Weather Integration → Risk Assessment → Recommendation → Data Logging

> DGA focuses strongly on **DECISION SUPPORT**, rather than merely returning a disease label.

---

## 🎯 4. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

### ⚡ Short-term Objectives
- Early detection of dangerous pests and diseases in durian trees.
- Significantly reduce manual farm inspection time and costs.
- Help digitize and manage data for individual trees.

### 🚀 Long-term Objectives
- Build a standard AI Operating System for smart durian farms.
- Standardize crop data for traceability and compliance.
- Assist agricultural enterprises in managing multiple large-scale farms simultaneously.

---

## 💎 6. PROJECT PHILOSOPHY (DIFFERENTIATION)

Durian Guardian AI does not stop at answering:
> ❌ *"What disease does this tree have?"*

Instead, DGA answers critical management questions:
- ✅ **Which tree or zone needs inspection first?**
- ✅ **What is the current risk level (%)?**
- ✅ **Why did the AI assign this risk level?** (AI Explainability)
- ✅ **What is the recommended priority action?**
